Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

You can open a trading account by clicking on the "Sign Up" button on our login page. You'll need to provide your PAN card, Aadhaar card, bank details, and complete the KYC process. The entire process takes about 15 minutes and your account will be activated within 24 hours.

Our customer support team is available Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. You can reach us via phone at 1800-123-4567 (toll-free) or email us at support@tradeeasy.com. For urgent trading queries during market hours, we also offer live chat support.

We offer competitive brokerage rates: Equity Delivery - 0.5% or ₹20 per trade (whichever is lower), Intraday & F&O - ₹20 per executed order, Mutual Funds - Zero commission on direct plans. There are no account opening charges or annual maintenance fees.

Your funds are held in segregated bank accounts and we use bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption for all transactions. We are registered with SEBI and follow all regulatory guidelines. Your personal data is protected under strict privacy policies and is never shared with third parties without consent.
